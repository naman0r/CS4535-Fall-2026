**10:00 timer:** 5 min demo · 4 min questions · 1 min to finish the worksheet. **Audience:** fill in a half-sheet for each team you watch, and each other team asks one question. The sheets go to the team at the end of class.

<aside className="notes">
**Draw the order now, and hand out the worksheets.**

**The question rule** keeps the room from going quiet: each other team owes one question per demo, so three student questions, then yours. A good question names something specific from the demo. "What happens if the scan is rotated?" works. "Cool, how long did that take?" doesn't count.

**Your question for each team** should come from the sprint artifacts. The one most worth asking is usually: what did the sprint change about your plan?

## Your First Implementation Ticket

<div style={{fontSize: '0.58em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

<div style={{backgroundColor: 'rgba(42,157,143,0.12)', padding: '0.5em', borderRadius: '8px', marginBottom: '0.5em'}}>

**XL. Know it before you leave today. Scope agreed with me on the issue by Mon Oct 19. Review-ready Thu Oct 29. Merged Wed Nov 4.**

- **Default: your own project.** Start from your sprint artifact, your team's filed issues, or your Agents Activity analysis
- **Already claimed an XL pool ticket in the Agents Activity?** That's your ticket
- **Pool:** two XL tickets left, #467 and #1026. Comment to claim

</div>

**XL means** more than one layer, and a one-sentence "done" that mentions a user. Steps 4 to 6 of the Agents Activity are how you scope it.

**Review, then it ships.** On Oct 29 I assign two reviewers to each stack, one teammate and one from another team. Reviews are due Nov 1, and you answer within 48 hours. Then I merge, and you go look: click it in your team's demo class, check Sentry, and post the verification in your team channel.

</div>
