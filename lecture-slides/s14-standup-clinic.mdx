## Standup #2

<div style={{fontSize: '0.62em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

<div style={{backgroundColor: 'rgba(42,157,143,0.12)', padding: '0.6em', borderRadius: '8px', marginBottom: '0.5em'}}>

**One team at a time. About 90 seconds each.**

1. **What your sprint found**, in one sentence a teammate can use. Point at the artifact in `notes/`.
2. **What it changed.** Did your team's plan or first milestone move because of it?
3. **What's blocking you**, and who you need it from.

</div>

<p style={{fontSize: '0.9em'}}>
Then the team answers the question from both meetings: <strong>does anything we've learned change our plan?</strong>
</p>

</div>

<aside className="notes">
**Thirteen people at 90 seconds is about 20 minutes.** Keep a timer visible. Cut off anyone reading their artifact aloud and ask for the one sentence.

**Push on point 1.** "I learned a lot about the OCR pipeline" isn't a finding. "The matcher fails on rotated scans, and here's the threshold" is.

**Point 2 is where scope gets set.** The checkpoint table says Checkpoint 1 is "the risks you found, the scope you've set." If a team's answer to "does it change our plan" is no across the board, ask what the sprint was for.

**Write blockers on the board** as they come up. They're the clinic queue.

**Attendance:** standup #2 is the sprint's in-person Pass item. Note who's absent.

> **Transition:** You've met your teams' findings. Now meet your function.
</aside>
